A few important details.

Are these real payments or real fraud verdicts?

No. PayShield is a prototype with simulated payments and synthetic training records. It does not connect to banks or police databases, and it cannot guarantee scam-free payments.

How are scores calculated?

The engine uses account age, KYC status, complaints and confirmed signals, payment activity, unusual behaviour, device/network signals and linked entities when data is available. In this demo, phone numbers use 10 and QR payments use 80. An active confirmed investigator finding overrides those profiles with risk of at least 95.

Does a QR score of 80 mean the receiver is a scammer?

No. It is a fixed demonstration value, not evidence of wrongdoing. A report is an allegation. Investigator findings here concern fictional evidence only.

Can I use “Hey Google” to send money?

The current app has an in-app voice payment flow. System-wide Google Assistant integration and real bank transfers are not connected. Saying approve still requires supported Android strong biometrics.

Is the investigator dossier private?

The website requires demo login. Sender PDFs exclude the detailed dossier. All IP, MAC, phone, location and KYC records shown are fictional training data—not information discovered about a real receiver. Production use would require individual access controls and authorized data partners.
